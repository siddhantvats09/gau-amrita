Hamari Kahani · Our Story

It started with Dadi Ji.

Three generations ago, Dadi Ji would wake before sunrise, collect fresh A2 milk from our beloved Gir cows and buffalos, and begin the sacred Bilona process with her own hands.

Today, we honour that same tradition — unchanged, uncompromised. Farm fresh, sealed in glass, and shipped straight from our village in Uccha Majra to your kitchen. No middleman, no compromise.
